The method

Three phases, twelve weeks, one coach the whole way through.

Weeks 1–3

Rest

Sleep, boundaries, and stepping back — with a coach helping you rest without guilt.

Weeks 4–8

Reset

Weekly sessions to find what depleted you, and what actually restores you.

Weeks 9–12

Return

A paced plan for going back to work, with limits you set before you're back in it.
